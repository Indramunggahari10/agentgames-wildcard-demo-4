Wallet Guard

Paste the three fields your wallet shows before signing — recipient, value, calldata — and get a plain-language risk verdict.

Read-only. This tool never signs, sends, or simulates a transaction, never touches funds, and never asks for keys. Nothing you paste leaves your browser except to this server for analysis, and nothing is stored or logged.

Checks: unlimited approvals, unverified spenders, plain transfers, risky selectors, unknown functions, and malformed calldata. A "safe" verdict means no flag was raised — it is not a guarantee. See the repo README for limits.
